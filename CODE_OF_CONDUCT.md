# Community conduct

GoingRusting welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, and other community spaces managed by GoingRusting.

## Treat people with respect

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; keep criticism specific and useful.
- Be patient with questions, and respect differences in experience and perspective.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal information without permission.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, and personal attacks.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to **trefilovvasylnpm@gmail.com**. Include links or context that help explain what happened. Avoid posting sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will consider the available context and may remove content, request a change in behavior, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and protect the community's ability to collaborate.
